The author recorded a Drift 2 go-kart with a 6.5 HP engine.
Public high quality preview used as source.
Conversions: mono PCM 44.1 kHz, high-pass 45 Hz, low-pass 4900 Hz,
cropping, loop crossfades and peak normalization.
Ralenti: source 193.30 to 194.55 seconds, 0.16 s crossfade.
En charge: source 198.15 to 198.83 seconds, 0.11 s crossfade.

The kart mesh and the original paint come from the team's existing project.
The new material is a locally adjusted color and metallic finish.
